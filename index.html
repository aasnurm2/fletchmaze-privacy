1. Introduction

Ludovarn Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use Fletchmaze, a mobile puzzle game.

The game is designed to store gameplay progress, settings, scores, virtual currency and other gameplay-related information locally on your device.

The current version does not require users to create a personal account to play.

Fletchmaze is designed to support advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements.

When advertising services are enabled, advertising providers such as Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, diagnostic information and other data necessary to provide advertisements, prevent fraud and measure advertising performance.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent will be requested before processing data for advertising.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases and subscriptions when these features are enabled. Payments are processed through Google Play. Ludovarn Games does not directly receive or store your payment card details.

5. Future Features

Future versions may introduce Google Play Games authentication, Facebook login, cloud saves, achievements and online leaderboards.

This Privacy Policy will be updated before such features are made available to users to explain the additional information processed and the available privacy controls.

Ludovarn Games is committed to protecting children's privacy. If features intended for children are introduced, they will be subject to applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies.

Locally stored gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to device backup settings.

Information processed by third-party service providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

Users may contact Ludovarn Games regarding access, correction or deletion of personal information.
